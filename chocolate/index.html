The finest in
the world.

In Japan, gifting fruit is a deeply rooted tradition expressing high respect and gratitude. These fruits are hand-grown with meticulous care to achieve flawless shapes, perfect sweetness and uniform colouring. They are elegantly boxed and presented like fine jewellery or vintage wine. It's always such a pleasure seeing customers go from unconvinced to completely in awe after trying our Pione Grapes. No need to go to Harrods, we have a selection of highly sought after Japanese luxury fruits right here! From Crown melons selling at £115 each to Miyazaki mangoes – a pair of which sold at auction for £4,000.

The Crown Melon is a hand-massaged, single-stem musk melon grown in Hokkaido or Shizuoka, famous for its perfectly round shape and rich melt-in-your-mouth sweetness. The Pione Grape is highly prized in Japan, frequently gifted in elaborate, cushioned boxes. The Miyazaki Mango also known as Taiyo no Tamago meaning Egg of the Sun, is a ruby-red mangoes, classified by hitting a strict weight and sugar thresholds. It is highly prized for its intensely sweet, almost candy-like flavour, completely fibreless flesh, and a creamy, melting texture. The Fuyu Persimmon is a traditional autumn fruit given for its crisp, sweet and non-astringent flesh.

Japanese Fruits

Muskmelons

Japanese muskmelons are some of the finest melons in the world, cultivated with extraordinary care to achieve perfectly rounded shapes, intricate netting and exceptional sweetness. In premium production, growers may leave just a single fruit on each plant, allowing it to receive the full benefit of the plant's nutrients. Each melon is carefully tended throughout its growth, with the fruit rotated and individually cared for to encourage beautifully even colouring and netting. Beneath the delicate, lace-like skin is pale, incredibly juicy flesh with a rich honey-like sweetness and a wonderfully soft, melting texture. Their combination of appearance, fragrance and flavour makes a perfectly grown Japanese muskmelon a true luxury fruit, traditionally presented as an extravagant and deeply thoughtful gift.

Crown Melons

Shizuoka Crown Melons are among the most prestigious fruits Japan has to offer, grown individually with an almost obsessive level of care and precision. Each plant produces a single melon, allowing the grower to concentrate its energy into producing one exceptionally beautiful fruit. The result is the unmistakable perfectly round shape, intricate netted skin and distinctive T-shaped stem associated with premium Crown Melons. Inside is pale green flesh that is extraordinarily juicy, fragrant and sweet, with a smooth, almost melting texture that makes the fruit feel more like a dessert than an ordinary melon. Their remarkable consistency, delicate aroma and rich honey-like sweetness have made Crown Melons a symbol of Japanese luxury, often presented in beautiful boxes as gifts for the most important occasions.

Fuyu Persimmons

Fuyu persimmons are beautiful Japanese autumn fruits, loved for their brilliant golden-orange colour, crisp texture and naturally sweet, honeyed flavour. Unlike astringent persimmons, Fuyu can be enjoyed while still firm, giving them a wonderfully satisfying crunch before releasing their juicy, delicate sweetness. Their flavour is beautifully clean and mellow, with subtle floral notes and very little acidity, while allowing them to soften brings an even more tender and luscious texture. Carefully grown Japanese Fuyu persimmons are particularly prized for their flawless appearance and rich colour, making them a beautiful seasonal fruit and a traditional choice for thoughtful gifting.

Pione Grapes

Pione grapes are magnificent Japanese grapes, famous for their enormous deep-purple berries, remarkable sweetness and incredibly juicy, almost gelatinous texture. Sometimes described as Japan's "Black Pearls", their striking appearance makes them particularly impressive when presented as a luxurious bunch. Each grape bursts with rich sweetness, balanced by refreshing acidity and delicate muscat-like and floral notes. Modern seedless Pione grapes are carefully cultivated to retain their impressive size while being wonderfully easy to enjoy. Their combination of huge berries, intense flavour, beautiful colour and meticulous cultivation has made them one of Japan's most sought-after luxury grapes and a particularly spectacular fruit to give as a gift.

Shine Muscat Grapes

Shine Muscat grapes are one of Japan's most celebrated luxury grape varieties, instantly recognisable by their large, luminous green berries and beautiful muscat fragrance. They are wonderfully crisp when bitten, with thin, edible skins and an abundance of exceptionally sweet, fragrant juice. Their flavour is rich and honeyed with delicate floral and muscat notes, balanced by a refreshing acidity that makes them incredibly moreish. The grapes are carefully grown and selected for their size, colour, sweetness and appearance, resulting in bunches that look almost like clusters of jewels. Beautiful to look at and extraordinary to eat, premium Shine Muscats perfectly capture the Japanese approach to producing fruit that is both delicious and almost impossibly beautiful.

Miyazaki Mangoes

Miyazaki mangoes are among the world's most luxurious mangoes, prized for their intensely sweet, almost confectionery flavour, extraordinary fragrance and smooth, completely fibreless flesh. Grown in carefully controlled conditions in Miyazaki Prefecture, the finest fruits are nurtured until they reach a perfect balance of colour, sweetness and ripeness. The golden flesh is incredibly creamy and juicy, with a soft, melting texture and a rich tropical flavour that can seem almost like mango jelly. As soon as the fruit is cut, its beautiful perfume fills the air, adding to the experience. The most prestigious fruits can be selected as Taiyo no Tamago, meaning "Egg of the Sun", a designation reserved for mangoes that meet exceptionally strict standards for size, sweetness and appearance. With their vibrant ruby-red skin, extraordinary flavour and remarkable rarity, Miyazaki mangoes have become an icon of Japanese luxury fruit.

Sun Fuji Apples

Japanese Sun Fuji apples are wonderfully crisp, exceptionally juicy and naturally sweet, offering an extraordinary level of flavour and freshness from such a familiar fruit. Carefully cultivated to develop beautiful colour and concentrated sweetness, they have a satisfying crunch followed by an abundance of juice and delicate honeyed notes. Their flavour is beautifully balanced, combining deep natural sweetness with just enough refreshing acidity to keep every bite bright and moreish. The flesh is firm, clean and incredibly juicy, while the carefully selected appearance makes premium Japanese Fuji apples as beautiful to present as they are delicious to eat. They are a wonderful example of how Japanese growers can take an everyday fruit and elevate it into something truly special.
