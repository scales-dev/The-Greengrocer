An exciting development

Chocolate room

This new addition to the shop will be a space dedicated entirely to the wonders of chocolate; a place where craftsmanship, indulgence and discovery come together under one roof. As an extension of the shop, the chocolate room is gradually taking shape as we continue to expand what we can offer our customers. Our vision is to create an immersive experience centred around some of the finest chocolate the world has to offer, bringing together exceptional producers and unique creations from Japan and beyond.

We intend to partner with a fantastic Japanese chocolate and cake company, alongside a specialist producer of luxury chocolate bonbons, to create a carefully curated selection of extraordinary chocolates, cakes and confections. From beautifully crafted individual pieces to rich, intricate creations, everything will be chosen for its quality, flavour and attention to detail.

But this space will be about more than simply buying chocolate. We plan to create and serve some of the most decadent liquid chocolate drinks imaginable, made with exceptional chocolate and designed to be enjoyed slowly and indulgently. The aim is to make every visit feel like a treat, whether you are discovering a new chocolate, enjoying a drink or simply taking in the atmosphere.

Ultimately, we want the chocolate room to become an unforgettable part of the shop; a warm, luxurious space where chocolate is at the heart of everything. From the moment you enter, the aroma of freshly prepared chocolate fills the room, creating an experience that is as memorable for the senses as it is for the palate.

It is our ambition to create somewhere truly special: a destination for chocolate lovers, a showcase for exceptional producers and a place where the simple pleasure of chocolate can be celebrated in its finest forms.
