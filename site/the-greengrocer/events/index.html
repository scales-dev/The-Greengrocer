Meet the team

Our events are an opportunity to meet the people behind The Greengrocer and share our passion for exceptional produce. We are always happy to talk fruit, answer questions and share our knowledge.

More than simply serving exceptional fruit, we aim to create an experience our guests will remember. From the produce itself to the way it is presented and enjoyed, every detail is considered.
